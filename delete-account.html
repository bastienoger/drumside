Delete your DrumSide account and data

You can delete your DrumSide account and everything tied to it at any time: from inside the app in seconds, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Effective 25 September 2026 · Applies to DrumSide on Android and iOS

In the app · immediate

Delete from DrumSide

The fastest way. Your account is removed right away.

  1. Open DrumSide and sign in
  2. Tap your profile picture, top right of Home
  3. Scroll to Account → Delete account
  4. Confirm. You may be asked to re-enter your password first
By email · within 7 days

Delete without the app

Lost your phone, or already uninstalled? Email us and we'll delete it for you.

  1. Email drumsideapp@outlook.com
  2. Write from the address on your account, or tell us your display name
  3. Ask us to delete your account
  4. We delete it and confirm by email within 7 days

What gets deleted

Both methods remove the same things.

Deleted

  • Your email, password and display name
  • Your Google account link, if you signed in that way
  • Grooves and fills you shared to the Community, and your votes
  • The link between your account and your Premium subscription record

Kept or not affected

  • Purchase records that tax and accounting law require us or the app stores to keep, for the legally required period
  • Reports you sent about other patterns, kept up to 12 months to handle abuse, without your name
  • Practice history, streaks and saved patterns on your device. These were never on our servers; uninstalling the app deletes them
  • Your Premium subscription itself. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store, or you'll keep being billed

Delete some data, keep your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove data.

  • A shared groove: open it in the Community tab and choose Remove
  • Anything else (for example your votes): email us and we'll remove it within 7 days

Request by email

No account or app needed. Just send us a message.
