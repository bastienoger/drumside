05 Community rules

The Community lets drummers share grooves and fills and vote on them. Anything you share is visible to every DrumSide user, with your display name.

What you can share

  • Patterns you created yourself, or traditional patterns anyone may use.
  • Titles and descriptions that are respectful and about drumming.

What is not allowed

  • Hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or otherwise offensive titles or content.
  • Spam, advertising, or links to other services.
  • Content that infringes someone else's rights, including transcriptions of copyrighted songs presented as your own.
  • Impersonating another person, or manipulating votes.

Your content

You keep the rights to the patterns you share. By sharing one, you let us store it, show it, and let other DrumSide users play, view, save and practise it in the app, for as long as it stays shared. You can remove it at any time from the Community screen.

Reports and moderation

Use Report on any pattern that breaks these rules; it is hidden for you straight away and we review it. We may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break these rules, without notice when the breach is serious. If you think we made a mistake, write to us.

06 Practice safely

  • Drums and headphones can be loud enough to damage your hearing. Wear hearing protection when you play a real kit, and keep headphone volume moderate.
  • Warm up, keep a relaxed grip, and take breaks. Stop if you feel pain, numbness or strain, and see a doctor if it continues.
  • DrumSide is a practice tool, not medical or professional advice.
