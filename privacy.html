Your data, in plain terms

DrumSide is a drum practice app. This page explains what it collects, what stays only on your phone, why we use each piece of data, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

Effective 16 October 2026 · Applies to the DrumSide app on Android and iOS

In short

  • Practice works entirely offline. Without a profile, your saved patterns, favorites, tempos and streaks stay on your device. With a profile, they are also backed up to your account so you can restore them on a new phone. You can turn this off.
  • A profile is optional. If you create one, we store your email and display name so your account, purchases and shared grooves stay tied to you.
  • Sharing a groove in the Community makes that pattern and your display name visible to other DrumSide users.
  • DrumSide Premium is billed by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • No ads, no analytics, no ad tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time, from the app or by email.

03 What stays on your device, and your backup

Everything you'd use without creating a profile works with no network access at all.

  • Your saved grooves, fills and exercises, and anything you write in the Studio
  • Favorites, tempo settings, click and count-in preferences, and theme
  • Practice minutes, skill levels, your daily streak and titles earned
  • The last exercise you opened, so DrumSide can offer to continue it

Without a profile, we can't read this data, back it up, or recover it if you lose your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

With a profile, DrumSide backs this data up to your account (Cloud Firestore), so it comes back when you sign in on a new phone or after reinstalling. Only your account can read the backup. You can turn backup off in Profile → Backup; the data then stays on your phone only. Deleting your account deletes the backup.

04 Your account

A profile is optional. It lets your Premium subscription and community activity follow you if you change phones.

Signing up with email stores that email address, a display name you choose, and a securely hashed password, handled by Google's Firebase Authentication. Signing up with Google shares your Google account's name, email and profile photo with us.

We use this only to identify your account, show your display name on grooves you share, and send account email (verification, password reset). We never send marketing email.

05 Community grooves

The Community tab lets drummers share grooves and fills and vote on them. Sharing something makes it visible to every DrumSide user.

  • A shared pattern includes its notation, your display name, and the votes it receives
  • Anyone in the app can play, view or vote on a pattern you share
  • You can remove your own shared pattern at any time from the Community screen
  • Reporting a pattern sends its ID and your reason to us. Other users never see who reported it

06 Payments and DrumSide Premium

Subscriptions are billed entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store. DrumSide never handles or stores your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check which subscription is active on your account across devices. RevenueCat receives an app user ID and your DrumSide purchase history. If you're signed in, that ID is your DrumSide account ID, so Premium can follow you to a new phone. RevenueCat never receives your name, email or card number from us.

You manage or cancel your subscription in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings. The Premium screen in the app links there.

08 How long we keep it

  • Account data (email, display name, sign-in details): until you delete your account.
  • Shared grooves and votes: until you remove them or delete your account.
  • Reports: until they're reviewed, then up to 12 months to handle repeat abuse, then deleted.
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat: while your account exists, then as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Account backup: until you delete your account. Turning backup off stops updating it.
  • On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

When you delete your account, your account data, backup, shared grooves and votes are removed from our systems. Requests by email are handled within 7 days.

11 Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can use these rights. If you're in the EU or UK, the GDPR guarantees them.

  • Access and portability: get a copy of the account data we hold, in a common format
  • Correction: fix your display name in the app, or ask us to correct anything else
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app, or ask us by email
  • Objection and restriction: object to, or ask us to limit, uses based on our legitimate interest
  • Complaint: you can complain to your data protection authority. In France, that's the CNIL (cnil.fr)

Delete your account

Profile → Account → Delete account removes your sign-in, email, display name, shared grooves and votes from our systems. You can also ask by email. See the account deletion page for details.

Delete a shared pattern

Open it from the Community tab and choose Remove. It disappears from every user's app.

Delete everything local

Uninstalling DrumSide removes all on-device data. We never received it, so there's nothing for us to delete.

We answer rights requests within one month.

12 Children's privacy

DrumSide is for a general audience learning drums and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. In France, if you're under 15, ask a parent or guardian before creating a profile (in other countries, check your local age). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

13 Security and changes

Account data is stored with Firebase's standard encryption in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute protection.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also tell you inside the app.
